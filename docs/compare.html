認錯公司比認不出公司貴。以下取自 STATE.campaign.traps。Naming the wrong company costs more than naming none. Taken from STATE.campaign.traps.

  • same-name-company

    A Yotta Infrastructure Solutions (Taiwan)

    B Yotta Data Services (India)

    -"Yotta Data Services" -India -Hiranandani

  • ex-employer

    A speaker's current employer on this edition's roster

    B employer named in an older bio, talk, or press release

    restrict to the current edition roster; an ex-employer is not evidence of presence

  • same-surname

    A two speakers sharing a surname at the same event

    B a third person of the same surname in the wider industry

    match on full name plus employer id, never on surname alone

  • name-collision

    A company named inside a session abstract or a partner slide

    B company on the official sponsor or exhibitor list

    a mention is not a presence; require sponsors.json or an explicit exhibitor capture

  • same-name-company

    A Lambda (Lambda Labs, GPU cloud, Gold sponsor)

    B AWS Lambda (serverless compute; AWS is a Platinum sponsor at the same show)

    "Lambda Labs" GPU cloud -"AWS Lambda" -serverless

  • name-collision

    A Ray (the open-source distributed framework) / Ray Summit

    B Ray as a personal given name, Ray-Ban, ray tracing

    Ray Anyscale distributed framework -"Ray-Ban" -"ray tracing"
