D 登記簿D register

每一次現場對話結束後就地登記三格。空著的格子寫 GAP 和你缺什麼，不要寫「沒有」——沒問到和沒有是兩件事。Fill the three cells the moment a conversation ends. An empty cell gets GAP and what is missing, never "none": not asked and not there are different findings.
